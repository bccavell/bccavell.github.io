Technical founders can build the product. Explaining it clearly is a different skill.

You know exactly why your product matters - you just built it. But translating that into a message a buyer immediately gets is a different craft, and it's easy to burn weeks second-guessing the homepage copy instead of shipping.

Hiring a full-time PMM this early is a big bet. Hiring a consultant usually means a deck and a Google Doc land in your inbox, everyone nods, and six months later the product has moved on but the messaging hasn't - because the person who wrote it is gone and nobody else knows how to update it.

The Sprint

Three weeks. One clear deliverable: messaging your team can actually run with.

Week 1

Buyer persona & VoC synthesis

Built from real customer interviews - not invented personas. We talk to your actual users and pull out the language they use to describe the problem you solve.

Week 2

Core messaging matrix

Your value proposition, messaging pillars, and a feature-to-benefit translation your whole team can use consistently - in sales calls, on the site, in decks.

Week 3

Copywriting sandbox

Homepage copy and sales one-liners, drafted and refined with you, ready to ship.

Delivered as strategy documents and a configured system your team keeps using after the engagement ends - more on that below.

The IP lives in your workspace, not mine.

Instead, the messaging strategy gets built into a Claude Skill configured inside your own Claude workspace - not mine.
